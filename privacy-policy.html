Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 30, 2026

Welcome to SimpleTick! Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the SimpleTick mobile application handles information when you use our app. By using SimpleTick, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. About SimpleTick

SimpleTick is a task management and productivity application that helps users organize their daily activities, manage tasks, set priorities, and track completed and pending tasks.

2. Information We Collect

SimpleTick is designed to store task information locally on your device. Depending on how you use the application, the following information may be stored:

  • Task titles and descriptions.
  • Task categories.
  • Task priorities.
  • Task due dates and times.
  • Task completion status.
  • Other information you enter into the application.

This information is stored in a local SQLite database using the Drift database library.

We do not collect or transmit your task information to our own external servers.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information stored in SimpleTick is used to provide the application's core functionality, including:

  • Creating, editing, and deleting tasks.
  • Organizing tasks by category and priority.
  • Displaying pending and completed tasks.
  • Tracking task deadlines and due dates.
  • Providing task reminders and notifications, if enabled.

4. Data Storage

Your task information is stored locally on your device using an SQLite database. The application does not require an online account to manage locally stored tasks.

If you uninstall SimpleTick or clear its application data, locally stored information may be permanently deleted.

Any device-level backup or restoration is controlled by your device and operating system settings.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your locally stored task information with third parties.

We do not operate a server that receives your task information.

6. Notifications

SimpleTick may use local notifications to remind users about their tasks and scheduled activities, if this feature is enabled.

Notifications are generated on your device. You can manage notification permissions through your device settings.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information handled by the application. Task data is stored locally on your device.

However, no method of electronic storage is completely secure. We recommend keeping your device protected with an appropriate screen lock and security updates.

8. Third-Party Services

The application is designed to operate without requiring third-party advertising or analytics services. If third-party services are introduced in future versions, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain their data practices.

9. Children's Privacy

SimpleTick does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The application is not specifically designed to collect children's personal information.

10. Your Privacy Choices

You can manage your task information directly within the application. You can edit or delete tasks whenever you wish.

You can also disable notifications through your device settings.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect application improvements, new features, or changes in applicable requirements.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
